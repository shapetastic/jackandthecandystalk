Sweet questions ❓

Everything you might want to know before you book.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Mumbles, Swansea and cover the surrounding area, including the Gower, Neath and Port Talbot. If you're a little further out, just ask — we're happy to travel for the right event.

How much does candy floss machine hire cost?

Our Floss Boss party package is £20 per 10 people. That includes a friendly operator, a freshly spun candy floss each in blue raspberry, strawberry or vanilla, and full setup and cleanup. Get in touch for a quote tailored to your event.

How does it work on the day?

We arrive, set up the candy floss machine and spin fresh floss for your guests throughout the event, then pack everything away afterwards. All you need is access to a standard power socket.

Can you do both indoor and outdoor events?

Yes — birthday parties, school fairs, fêtes, weddings and community events, indoors or outdoors, as long as there's a standard power socket nearby and cover from the rain if we're outside.
