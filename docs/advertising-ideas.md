# JJ Entertainments — Advertising & Promotion Ideas

Practical ways to get more candy floss bookings around **Mumbles, Swansea and
the surrounding area**. This is broader than SEO (which is covered in
[`seo-next-steps.md`](./seo-next-steps.md)) — it's all the everyday marketing a
small, family-run business can do.

Nice start with the **flyers in local shops** 👏 — this list builds on that.
Ideas are grouped and roughly ordered **cheapest / highest-return first**. You
won't do all of them — pick a handful and keep them going.

> **Two things that make everything below work harder:**
> 1. Put the **same details** on everything — *JJ Entertainments · Mumbles,
>    Swansea · 07818 074565 · jjentertainments.co.uk*.
> 2. Make it **easy to book** — every flyer, post and card should point to the
>    website or phone number, ideally with a **QR code** to the site.

---

## 1. Free & local (do these first) — 💷 free · ⏱️ low effort

- **More flyers & cards, in the right places.** Beyond shops, ask to leave a
  small stack or pin a card at: soft-play centres, cafés with family areas,
  children's hairdressers, libraries, community centres, village halls, church
  halls, leisure centres, nurseries and pre-schools, dance/gymnastics clubs, and
  supermarket community noticeboards (Tesco, Co-op, etc.).
- **Add a QR code to the flyer** that opens the website. Free to generate; lets
  people book on the spot with their phone. (Ask your developer, or use any free
  QR generator pointing to `https://jjentertainments.co.uk`.)
- **Car magnet / window sticker.** A cheap magnetic sign on the car with the
  name, number and website turns every trip and every parked event into an advert.
- **Banner at events.** A small roll-up or vinyl banner at every booking so
  guests know who to call — parties are where your next customers are watching.
- **Word of mouth, deliberately.** Hand every customer **2–3 business cards** at
  the end of an event ("pass these to anyone planning a party"). Referrals are the
  #1 source of bookings for businesses like this.
- **Community noticeboards online.** Post in local Facebook groups — Mumbles /
  Swansea parents & mums groups, "What's on in Swansea", local buy/sell/recommend
  groups, and Nextdoor. Follow each group's advertising rules (some have a set
  day for business posts).

## 2. Social media — 💷 free · ⏱️ ongoing, little and often

- **Set up Facebook & Instagram** (also in the SEO doc). This is where party
  planning happens locally.
- **Post real photos & short videos** — the machine spinning floss is genuinely
  eye-catching in a video. A few seconds of fresh floss being wound = great
  content. (Get permission for anything showing children.)
- **Post consistently, not perfectly** — one post or story a week beats a big
  burst then nothing.
- **Encourage tags & shares** — ask happy customers to tag you; reshare their
  photos. Each share reaches a new local family for free.
- **Run the odd giveaway** — e.g. "tag a friend who's got a party coming up to
  win a free candy floss upgrade." Cheap, spreads fast locally.

## 3. Partnerships & referrals — 💷 free / swap · ⏱️ medium

- **Team up with other party suppliers** — bouncy castle hire, face painters,
  balloon artists, kids' entertainers, mobile disco, party venues. Recommend each
  other; you're not competitors, you complete the same party. Ask to be on their
  "recommended suppliers" list (and list them back).
- **Wedding & event venues** — get onto their supplier/recommendations list;
  weddings and corporate fun days are higher-value bookings.
- **Schools & PTAs** — after a school fair, ask to be mentioned in the newsletter
  or on the PTA page. Offer a small discount for PTA-organised events.
- **Referral reward** — "recommend us and get £X off your next booking, and so
  do they." Gives existing customers a reason to spread the word.

## 4. Reviews & reputation — 💷 free · ⏱️ 2 min per customer

- **Ask every happy customer for a Google review** (see the SEO doc). Reviews
  win you *both* higher Google ranking **and** more bookings — people trust a
  business with lots of recent 5-stars.
- **Collect a few photo testimonials** ("the kids loved it!") to reshare on
  social and add to the website later.

## 5. Low-cost paid options — 💷 small budget · ⏱️ medium

Only once the free stuff is running. Start small (£5–10/day) and see what works.

- **Facebook / Instagram ads** — the best-value paid option for a local business.
  You can target *parents within ~10 miles of Swansea*, which is exactly your
  customer. Boost your best-performing organic post to start.
- **Local print** — parish magazines, school newsletters, community mags and
  local event programmes are cheap and reach exactly the right people.
- **Google Ads (Local)** — bid on searches like *"candy floss hire Swansea"*.
  Can work but costs add up; the free Google Business Profile + SEO usually gives
  better return first. Try only if you have budget to spare.
- **Local sponsorship** — sponsor a kids' sports team, gala or school fair. Often
  gets your name on kit, banners or a programme, plus goodwill (and sometimes a
  website link → good for SEO too).
- **Leaflet drops** — a printer/distributor can post flyers to homes in chosen
  Swansea postcodes. Time it before party-heavy seasons.

## 6. Seasonal & event pushes — 💷 free/low · ⏱️ planned

Candy floss demand spikes at certain times — advertise ahead of them:

- **Summer** — fêtes, school fairs, garden parties, community galas.
- **Christmas / winter** — Christmas fairs, markets, work parties, Santa events.
- **Halloween & Easter** — themed events and school activities.
- **Wedding season** — spring/summer; target venues and planners.
- Post a "**Now taking bookings for [season] — dates going fast!**" message a few
  weeks before each, on social and in the shop flyers.

## 7. Nice extras — 💷 varies · ⏱️ low

- **Branded T-shirt / apron** for the operator at events — looks professional and
  advertises while you work.
- **A little sign by the machine** with the name, number and a QR code, so guests
  can book while they're enjoying the floss.
- **Loyalty for repeat customers** — nurseries, clubs and venues that book often;
  offer a small repeat-booking discount to keep them coming back.

---

## A simple monthly rhythm

You don't need to do everything. A sustainable routine:

1. **Every event:** hand out cards, ask for a review, take a photo/video.
2. **Weekly:** one social post or story (reuse an event photo).
3. **Monthly:** top up flyers in shops, post in 1–2 local Facebook groups, reach
   out to one new partner (venue, supplier or school).
4. **Before each season:** a "now taking bookings" push online and on flyers.

Keep the details identical everywhere, always point people to the website or
phone, and the bookings compound over time.
