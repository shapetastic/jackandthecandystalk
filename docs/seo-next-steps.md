# JJ Entertainments — SEO Next Steps (off-site work)

This is the to-do list for getting JJ Entertainments higher up Google. The
**on-site** work (the technical SEO built into the website) is done — see
"What's already done on the website" at the bottom.

Everything in **this** document has to be done by a person with a login; it
**can't be built into the website's code**. For a local business like candy
floss hire in Swansea, this off-site work is actually the *bigger* half of
ranking well — especially the Google Business Profile.

Work top-down: items are in priority order. Each has a ⭐ **priority**, a ⏱️
**effort**, and plain steps.

> **Golden rule — NAP consistency.** Everywhere you list the business online,
> write the **N**ame, **A**rea and **P**hone number *exactly* the same way:
> - **Name:** JJ Entertainments
> - **Area:** Swansea (and surrounding area)
> - **Phone:** 07818 074565
> - **Website:** https://jjentertainments.co.uk
> - **Email:** bookings@jjentertainments.co.uk
>
> Inconsistent details (e.g. "JJ Entertainment" vs "JJ Entertainments", or two
> different phone numbers) confuse Google and hurt local ranking.

## 1. Google Business Profile (GBP) — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ do this first ⏱️ 30–60 min + verification wait

**Why:** This is the single biggest lever for a local service business. It's what
puts you in Google Maps and the "local pack" (the map + 3 businesses that show at
the top of local searches like *"candy floss hire Swansea"*). Without it, you're
almost invisible for local searches no matter how good the website is.

**Steps:**
1. Go to <https://business.google.com> and sign in with a Google account you'll
   keep (ideally a dedicated business Gmail, not a personal one).
2. Enter the business name **JJ Entertainments**.
3. When asked "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?" choose **No**
   — this makes it a **service-area business** (correct for mobile candy floss
   hire; your home address stays private).
4. Set the **service areas**: Swansea, plus the Gower, Mumbles, Neath, Port
   Talbot and anywhere else you'll travel.
5. **Primary category:** try "Party equipment rental service" or "Children's
   party service". **Additional categories:** add "Caterer" / "Event planner"
   if relevant. (Categories strongly affect what searches you show up for.)
6. Add the **phone** (07818 074565) and **website**
   (https://jjentertainments.co.uk).
7. Add **hours** (or set "open by appointment").
8. **Verify** the profile — Google will ask to confirm by phone, text, video or
   postcard. Follow the prompt. You can't rank in Maps until verified.
9. Once live: add **photos** (see item 9), write a short **"from the business"**
   description mentioning *candy floss machine hire in Swansea*, and add your
   **Floss Boss package (£20 per 10 people)** under **Products/Services**.
10. Start posting **GBP updates/offers** every week or two (a photo + a sentence).
    Active profiles rank better.

## 2. Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster Tools — ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ⏱️ 20 min

**Why:** These are Google's and Microsoft's free dashboards showing how your site
appears in search — what people searched, what pages showed, and any problems.
They also let you **submit your sitemap** so your pages get found faster.

**Steps:**
1. **Google Search Console:** <https://search.google.com/search-console>. Add
   property `https://jjentertainments.co.uk`.
   - Verify ownership. Easiest option: **DNS record** (add a TXT record in your
     Host Papa / domain DNS settings). Alternatively Google gives you a small
     **HTML meta tag** — send it to your developer and it can be dropped into the
     site's `<head>` (there's a placeholder ready in the site for this).
   - Once verified, go to **Sitemaps** and submit: `sitemap.xml`.
2. **Bing Webmaster Tools:** <https://www.bing.com/webmasters>. You can import
   directly from Google Search Console in one click, then submit the sitemap
   there too.
3. Check back monthly for the searches bringing people to the site — great clues
   for what to mention more of on the page.

## 3. Bing Places for Business — ⭐⭐⭐ ⏱️ 15 min

**Why:** The Bing equivalent of Google Business Profile. Smaller audience, but
quick to do and it also feeds some AI assistants and Windows/Outlook search.

**Steps:**
1. Go to <https://www.bingplaces.com>.
2. You can often **import** everything straight from your Google Business Profile
   — do that if offered.
3. Otherwise fill in the same NAP details as GBP (keep them identical).

## 4. Local directories & listings (citations) — ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ⏱️ 1–2 hrs, spread over time

**Why:** Each reputable directory that lists your business with consistent NAP is
a "citation" — a vote of confidence that tells Google you're a real, established
local business. This is a core local-ranking factor.

**General UK directories:**
- Yell — <https://www.yell.com>
- Thomson Local — <https://www.thomsonlocal.com>
- FreeIndex — <https://www.freeindex.co.uk>
- Cylex UK — <https://www.cylex-uk.co.uk>
- Scoot — <https://www.scoot.co.uk>
- Yelp UK — <https://www.yelp.co.uk>

**Party / event marketplaces (very relevant — real enquiries come from these):**
- Add to Event — <https://www.addtoevent.co.uk>
- Poptop — <https://www.poptop.uk.com>
- Bark — <https://www.bark.com>
- Bidvine, Yumbles, or similar as you find them.

**Local Swansea / South Wales:**
- Search "Swansea business directory" and "South Wales events directory" and add
  the business to any that look legitimate.
- Local Facebook groups and community pages (see item 6).

**Rule:** Use the *exact same* name, area, phone and website every time. Keep a
simple spreadsheet of where you've listed so you can update them all if a detail
ever changes.

## 5. Reviews — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ongoing ⏱️ 2 min per customer

**Why:** Google reviews are a huge local-ranking **and** trust factor. A profile
with lots of recent, genuine 5-star reviews outranks and out-converts an empty
one. This is one of the highest-return habits you can build.

**Steps:**
1. After GBP is set up (item 1), Google gives you a short **"review link"** —
   find it in your GBP dashboard under "Ask for reviews".
2. Send it to **every happy customer** the day after their event: a quick text or
   WhatsApp — *"So glad the kids loved the candy floss! If you have a sec, a
   quick Google review really helps our little family business: [link]"*.
3. **Reply to every review**, good or bad — a friendly, professional reply.
   Google rewards engagement, and it looks great to prospective customers.
4. Aim for a steady trickle (a few a month) rather than a big burst.

## 6. Social media profiles — ⭐⭐⭐ ⏱️ 45 min setup, then ongoing

**Why:** Facebook and Instagram are where party planning happens locally. They
drive direct enquiries, give you a place to post event photos, and act as extra
trusted links back to the website.

**Steps:**
1. Create a **Facebook Business Page** and an **Instagram** account for
   JJ Entertainments. Use the same logo, NAP and website link.
2. Post event photos regularly (with permission for any showing children).
3. **Join local Facebook groups** — Swansea parents/mums groups, "what's on in
   Swansea", local buy/sell/recommend groups — and answer when people ask for
   party ideas or recommendations (follow each group's advertising rules).
4. **Send the profile URLs back to your developer.** They'll:
   - link them from the website footer, and
   - add them to the website's structured data (`sameAs`), which helps Google
     connect the profiles to your business. *(Placeholder is already in the code
     waiting for these links.)*

## 7. Local backlinks — ⭐⭐⭐ ongoing ⏱️ varies

**Why:** Links from other reputable local websites are one of the strongest
ranking signals. Quality and local relevance matter far more than quantity —
avoid spammy "buy 1000 links" services, which can get you penalised.

**Practical, safe ideas:**
- **Partner with local venues** — soft-play centres, village halls, wedding
  venues, marquee-hire firms, bouncy-castle hirers. Ask to be on their
  "recommended suppliers" page (and list them back).
- **Schools & PTAs** — after doing a school fair, ask if they'll mention/link you
  on their newsletter or PTA fundraising page.
- **Sponsor a local event or charity** — many list sponsors with a link.
- **Local press / community sites** — "This is South Wales", local news, or a
  Swansea "what's on" blog. A short story ("family-run candy floss business")
  can earn a link.
- **Wedding & party planners** — get onto their supplier lists.

## 8. Analytics (measure what's working) — ⭐⭐ ⏱️ 20 min

**Why:** So you can see how many people visit, what they search, and how many
send an enquiry — instead of guessing.

**Steps:**
1. Create a free **Google Analytics 4** property at
   <https://analytics.google.com>.
2. It will give you a **Measurement ID** (looks like `G-XXXXXXXXXX`).
3. **Send that ID to your developer.** They'll add the tracking snippet to the
   site and set the **Thank-you page as a conversion**, so you can see how many
   enquiries the site generates. *(Ready to wire in as soon as you have the ID.)*
